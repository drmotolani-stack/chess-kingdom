What did your child actually experience?

About 4 minutes · Best completed after three short sessions across a week. If your child stopped earlier, your feedback is just as useful.

A small, relaxed trial

Try one or two missions at a time, roughly 5–10 minutes if your child is comfortable. Stay nearby for younger children. Help when needed, use the “A grown-up helped” button, and stop if your child wants a break.

After the first visit, notice whether your child asks to return before you suggest it. There is no need to finish all three worlds.

Please describe what you observed, including boredom, confusion, or frustration. This pilot tests the experience and chess learning; it cannot establish improvements in general attention or intelligence.
